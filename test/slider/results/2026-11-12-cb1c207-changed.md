Delta: mean per-puzzle difference in moves (this row minus the comparison), negative = the player gets a shorter
solution. Min, p5, p50 (median), p95, Max: the spread of those differences; Min is the puzzle that gained most, Max
the one that lost most. Better/Worse/Equal: puzzles with a shorter/longer/same solution. 95% CI: confidence interval
of the mean. p: exact sign test on Better/Worse, the chance of a split this uneven if both were equally good.
pN: N% of the puzzles differ this much or less. Avg gain/loss: average difference of the better/worse puzzles.
Moves won/lost: the sum of those differences over all puzzles; won minus lost, divided by the puzzles, is Delta. CPU:
processor time of the whole solve (the player waits the solve time regardless). Tick: processor time of one game
tick, the cost per frame.
